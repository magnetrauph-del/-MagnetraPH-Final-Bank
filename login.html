FAQ
How do I log in to MagnetraPH?
Enter your registered email and password, then tap Log in. Your session is protected by AppCheck and device verification.
I forgot my password. What should I do?
Tap Forgot password, enter your email, and we will send a reset link. Check Inbox and Spam folder. Link expires in 1 hour.
Why is my login temporarily unavailable?
For your protection, login is paused after 3 incorrect attempts. Please wait 60 seconds and try again. This prevents brute-force attacks.
How does Log in with screen lock work?
If your phone has fingerprint, face unlock, or PIN, you can use it for faster login. Tap Log in with screen lock and verify with your biometrics. Secured by WebAuthn user verification.
Is my account safe from hackers?
Yes. MagnetraPH uses Firebase Auth, AppCheck, device ID binding, constant-time delay, and rate limiting. Even skilled hackers cannot bypass screen lock or brute-force your account.
Can I use Google to log in?
Yes. Tap Connect to Google and select your Google account. We will create or link your MagnetraPH profile securely.
