Help Center
How do I log in?
Enter your email and password then tap Log in.
I forgot my password. What should I do?
Tap Forgot password. Enter your email. Check Inbox and SPAM. Firebase will send reset link.
Why is my login temporarily unavailable?
For protection, login is paused for 30 seconds after 3 incorrect attempts.
How does Log in with screen lock work?
If phone has fingerprint or face unlock, you can log in faster. Enable in phone Settings.
The eye icon does not show my password?
Tap eye icon on right side of password field to show or hide password with blink animation.
I cannot continue with Google?
Allow pop ups in browser. Tap Continue with Google and choose account.
