Help Center
How do I log in?
Enter your email and password then tap Log in. Make sure your email is correct and your password has at least 6 characters.
I forgot my password. What should I do?
Tap Forgot password. Enter your email. Check your Inbox and SPAM folder. Open the email and create a new password.
Why is my login temporarily unavailable?
For your protection, login is paused for 30 seconds after several incorrect attempts. Please wait and try again.
How does Log in with screen lock work?
If your phone has fingerprint or face unlock, you can log in faster. Set it up in your phone Settings. It only works on the official website.
The eye icon does not show my password?
Tap the eye icon on the right side of the password field to show or hide your password.
I cannot continue with Google?
Please allow pop ups in your browser. Tap Continue with Google and choose your account.
