FAQ

About MagnetraPH

What is MagnetraPH?
MagnetraPH is a set of business tools for Filipino entrepreneurs, all in one app. You can start with ready-to-send follow-up emails for your customers, and more tools are on the way.
Can I use MagnetraPH on my phone and computer?
Yes. Open magnetra.app in Chrome or Safari on your phone or computer, and log in with the same account.

Account and login

How do I log in to MagnetraPH?
Enter your email and password, then tap Log in.
I forgot my password. What should I do?
Tap Forgot password, enter your email, and we will send you a reset link. Check your Inbox and Spam folder. The link expires in 1 hour.
Why can't I log in to my account?
Check your email and password, and make sure you have verified your email. For your security, login is paused for a short time after several incorrect attempts. Try again later.
I did not get the verification email.
Check your Spam folder, then log in and tap Send verification email again.
Can I use Google to log in?
Yes. Tap Continue with Google and choose your Google account.
How do I contact MagnetraPH support?
Email us at support@magnetra.app

Security and privacy

Is my MagnetraPH account safe?
We use verified sign-in and bot protection on secure servers. Never share your password with anyone, and log out when using a shared device.
Who can see my business data?
Your data is linked to your account and is not shown to other users. Our Privacy Policy explains what we collect and how we use it.
