Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

In short

  • Your account (your email, and your name if you sign in with Google) is kept by Google Firebase Authentication. MagnetraPH's own server keeps only your account ID and email.
  • Your Business Profile and settings are saved on your phone. Your Business Profile is erased when you log out.
  • What you make in the tools (banners, photos, quotations, customer names, messages) is not uploaded.
  • No ads and no analytics, and we don't sell your information. The only outside check is Google reCAPTCHA bot protection.
  • You can delete your account anytime in Settings.
  • Questions? Email support@magnetra.app.

Sa madaling salita

  • Ang account mo (email, at pangalan kung Google ang gamit mo) ay nasa Google Firebase Authentication. ID at email lang ng account ang nasa sariling server ng MagnetraPH.
  • Nasa phone mo ang Business Profile at mga setting mo. Nabubura ang Business Profile kapag nag-log out ka.
  • Hindi ina-upload ang mga gawa mo sa tools (banner, photo, quotation, pangalan ng customer, message).
  • Walang ads at walang analytics, at hindi namin ibinebenta ang impormasyon mo. Ang tanging check mula sa labas ay ang Google reCAPTCHA, panlaban sa bot.
  • Puwede mong burahin ang account mo anumang oras sa Settings.
  • May tanong? Mag-email sa support@magnetra.app.

Ang buong Privacy Policy sa ibaba ay nasa English, para eksakto ang legal na kahulugan.

MagnetraPH ("MagnetraPH", "we", "us") is a web app with business tools for Filipino entrepreneurs, at magnetra.app. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes, and the choices you have. It describes how the app works today. If that changes, we will update this page and the date above.

1. Who we are

MagnetraPH (Ultra Magnetic Traffic PH). For privacy questions or requests, email support@magnetra.app.

2. Information we handle

2.1 Your account

  • When you create an account with an email and password, your email address and password go from the app straight to Google Firebase Authentication. The app checks your new password's strength on your device, but MagnetraPH's server never receives or stores your password.
  • When you sign in with Google, Firebase Authentication receives your email address and basic Google profile information, such as your name and profile photo.
  • Firebase Authentication also keeps technical account records, such as your account ID, whether your email is verified, your sign-in method, and when your account was created and last used.
  • Firebase Authentication sends your verification and password-reset emails.
  • After your email is verified, MagnetraPH's server saves a profile with your account ID and email address in our database (hosted by Supabase). This is the only account record on our own server.

2.2 Security checks

  • To protect the app from bots and abuse, we use Firebase App Check with Google reCAPTCHA Enterprise. reCAPTCHA collects hardware and software information, such as device and app data, and sends it to Google for analysis. Google's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service apply to it.
  • Every request to our server is checked with your sign-in token and an App Check token. Our server takes your account ID only from the verified sign-in token.
  • Google (Firebase Hosting, Firebase Authentication, App Check, reCAPTCHA and the servers that deliver their code) and Cloudflare process technical information, such as your IP address, browser type and the time of a request, to deliver the app and keep it secure. Our own server code does not save this information.

2.3 Information saved on your device

The app saves some information in your browser's storage on your device, not on our server:

  • Your sign-in session, kept by Firebase Authentication in your browser, so you stay logged in until you log out or delete your account. It includes your email address (and, if you use Google, your name and profile photo).
  • Your Business Profile (business name and contact details), if you save one. It is erased when you log out.
  • Your preferences, such as language, appearance (light or dark), sounds, Friendly Care, and whether you have seen the welcome message or used the app on this device before. These stay on the device after you log out, so the app keeps the look you chose.
  • Short-lived session information: counts of finished tasks per tool (numbers only), whether the greeting and Friendly Care were shown, whether your server profile was set up, short wait timers after sending a verification or reset email, and, after you copy a quotation or open Print, the customer's name, the first item, the number of items and the total, so Follow-up Messages can offer them for up to 12 hours. Session information is cleared when you close the tab or log out; you can also remove the quotation details in Follow-up.
  • Security helpers, such as counters that briefly pause logins after several wrong passwords, and the time of your last activity. If the app stays open with no activity for 60 minutes, it logs you out. Closing the tab or browser does not log you out, so log out on shared devices.

2.4 What you create in the tools

Instant Banner, Quotation and Follow-up Messages work in your browser. Your text, photos, banners, quotations, customer names and messages are not uploaded to MagnetraPH. When you download, copy, print or share something, it goes where you choose (for example, your phone's files or a chat app), and you send it yourself. MagnetraPH never sends, posts or publishes anything for you.

2.5 When you contact us

If you email us or use Report a problem, we receive what you send: your email address, your message, and any device information you choose to include. Report a problem shows you exactly what device information will be included before you send it, and adds it only if you tick the box.

2.6 What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • We don't show ads.
  • We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools in the app. The only outside check is Google reCAPTCHA bot protection (section 2.2).
  • We don't handle payments. MagnetraPH does not hold, transfer or store your money.

3. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
  • To protect the app from bots, abuse and unauthorized access.
  • To answer your messages and reports, and to fix and improve the app.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

4. Service providers

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Hosting, App Check): sign-in, hosting the app, security checks.
  • Google reCAPTCHA Enterprise: bot protection.
  • Cloudflare (Workers): runs our server code.
  • Supabase: stores your account profile (account ID and email).
  • Our email service provider: receives the messages you send to support@magnetra.app.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Some of them may store or process information outside the Philippines.

5. How long we keep information

  • Your account and your server profile: until you delete your account.
  • Information on your device: as described in section 2.3. You can also clear it in your browser's settings.
  • Messages and reports you send us: only as long as needed to help you and to keep a record of the request.
  • Providers may keep technical logs for limited periods under their own policies.

6. Deleting your account

Go to Settings, then Delete account at the bottom. You confirm with your password, or by signing in with Google again. This deletes your MagnetraPH profile on our server (account ID and email) and your login in Firebase Authentication, and clears your Business Profile and session information from that device. It can't be undone.

Not deleted: files you already downloaded, copied or shared (they are yours, on your device or in your apps), emails you sent us, and your settings on the device (language, appearance, sound, Friendly Care, and whether you have seen the welcome message), which are not linked to your account. Provider logs follow their own retention.

7. Your rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), you have rights that include the right to be informed, to object, to access, to correct, to erasure or blocking, to data portability, to damages, and to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission. To use these rights, email support@magnetra.app. We may need to confirm that a request comes from you before acting on it.

8. Security

We use verified sign-in, email verification, bot protection, checks on every server request, and encrypted connections (HTTPS). No method of storing or sending information is completely secure, so we can't promise perfect security. Keep your password private, and log out on shared devices.

9. Children

MagnetraPH is made for people running a business. It is not intended for children.

10. Changes to this policy

When the app changes how it handles information, we will update this policy and the date at the top before the change applies.
